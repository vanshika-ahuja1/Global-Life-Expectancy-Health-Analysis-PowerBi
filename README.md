# 🌍 Global Life Expectancy & Health Insights – Power BI

This project is an interactive **Power BI dashboard** that analyzes global life expectancy trends, health risks, and development indicators across 193 countries (2000–2015).

---

## 📌 Objective

To understand how economic, health, and lifestyle factors impact life expectancy and global well-being.

---

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset includes:

- Life expectancy and mortality metrics
- GDP and population data
- Schooling and immunization rates
- BMI and alcohol consumption
- Development status & income categories

---

## 🛠️ Key Steps

- Data cleaning & transformation using **Power Query**
- Data modeling and relationships
- KPI creation with **DAX**
- Forecasting and key influencer analysis

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### Health Risk Analysis
- Life expectancy vs infant & adult mortality
- GDP vs life expectancy relationship
- Developed vs developing country comparison

### Global Development Insights
- Schooling and income category impact
- Population distribution by status
- Country-wise life expectancy leaders

### Health & Lifestyle Impact
- Alcohol consumption trends
- BMI and mortality risk analysis
- Life expectancy forecast

### Education & Immunization Insights
- Life expectancy by income level
- Polio & Diphtheria immunization comparison
- Education level impact
